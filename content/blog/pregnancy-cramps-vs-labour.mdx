> **Educational Medical Notice (`[DRAFT — REQUIRES CLIENT MEDICAL APPROVAL]`):** This guide is for general maternal education only. If you are pregnant and experiencing regular painful contractions, vaginal bleeding, fluid leakage, or reduced fetal movement, proceed immediately to your hospital maternity unit.

## Why Do Mild Cramps Happen During Normal Pregnancy?

Throughout pregnancy, the uterus grows from a small pear-sized organ into a muscular shelter supporting your growing baby. Because of this rapid growth, many expectant mothers experience mild, occasional sensations such as:

- **Round Ligament Stretching (Second Trimester):** Brief, sharp or pulling sensations along the sides of the lower abdomen when standing up quickly, coughing, or turning in bed.
- **Braxton Hicks ("Practice") Contractions:** Irregular, mild tightening of the abdomen that starts in the late second or third trimester as the uterine muscle prepares for childbirth.

## Braxton Hicks vs. True Labour Contractions: Key Differences

Understanding four simple clinical patterns helps you distinguish practice tightening from true labour:

1. **Timing & Regularity:**
   - *Braxton Hicks:* Irregular; they do not come at predictable intervals and do not get closer together.
   - *True Labour:* Regular and rhythmic (e.g., every 10 minutes, then every 5 minutes), lasting 45–60 seconds each.
2. **Response to Rest & Hydration:**
   - *Braxton Hicks:* Usually ease up when you drink a glass of water, empty your bladder, or change position and lie on your left side.
   - *True Labour:* Continue and grow stronger regardless of rest, hydration, or walking.
3. **Progression of Intensity:**
   - *Braxton Hicks:* Remain mild tightenings rather than progressively painful waves.
   - *True Labour:* Steadily increase in strength, duration, and frequency over time.
4. **Location of Pain:**
   - *Braxton Hicks:* Typically felt mainly across the front of the abdomen.
   - *True Labour:* Often begins in the lower back and wraps around to the lower abdomen, accompanied by pelvic pressure.

## Obstetric Warning Signs: When to Go to the Hospital Immediately

Never wait at home if abdominal pain or cramping is accompanied by any of these warning signs:

- **Vaginal bleeding or bright red spotting** at any stage of pregnancy.
- **Watery fluid leaking or gushing** from the vagina (rupture of membranes).
- **Regular contractions before 37 weeks** of pregnancy (preterm labour).
- **Noticeable decrease in your baby's kicks or movements.**
- **Severe, constant abdominal pain** that does not relax between contractions, or severe headache, blurred vision, and sudden facial/hand swelling.

## اردو خلاصہ: حمل کا عام کھنچاؤ بمقابلہ زچگی کا حقیقی درد

حمل کے دوران بچے دانی کے پھیلنے کی وجہ سے پیٹ کے نچلے حصے میں ہلکا کھنچاؤ محسوس ہونا ایک قدرتی عمل ہے، لیکن حقیقی زچگی کے درد کی پہچان بہت ضروری ہے:

- **فرضی کھنچاؤ (Braxton Hicks):** یہ بے قاعدہ ہوتا ہے، پانی پینے یا کروٹ بدل کر آرام کرنے سے ٹھیک ہو جاتا ہے اور وقت کے ساتھ تیز نہیں ہوتا۔
- **زچگی کا حقیقی درد (True Labour):** یہ باقاعدہ وقفے سے آتا ہے، کمر سے شروع ہو کر پیٹ کی طرف آتا ہے، چلنے پھرنے یا آرام کرنے سے ختم نہیں ہوتا بلکہ بتدریج تیز اور قریب ہوتا جاتا ہے۔
- **ہنگامی علامات:** اگر درد کے ساتھ خون آئے، پانی کی تھیلی پھٹ جائے (پانی خارج ہو)، بچے کی حرکت کم محسوس ہو یا 37 ہفتے سے پہلے باقاعدہ درد شروع ہو جائے تو فوراً ہسپتال کی ایمرجنسی سے رجوع کریں۔
